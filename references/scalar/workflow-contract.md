# Scalar sales-material workflow contract

This contract is shared by the Scalar product, proposal, account-plan, APS,
and AE-material skills.

- Read supplied minutes, CRM exports, ledgers, and customer material before
  asking questions.
- Use `sales-playbook.md` as the only source for phases, gates, and checkpoint
  definitions. Skills must not redefine them.
- Label facts, observations, and assumptions distinctly. Unknowns become
  questions or actions, never invented content.
- Keep customer-facing and internal material separate. Named-person opinions,
  competitor weaknesses, unconfirmed figures, and account-ledger internals are
  internal only.
- Follow `references/workflow-contract.md` for outline approval, offline
  validation, generation, QA, cleanup, and delivery.
- Snapshot before every in-place update. Preserve stable URLs for account-plan
  and APS deliverables.
- Never share `00_活動計画` or `90_社内` with a customer or partner.
